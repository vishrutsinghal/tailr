## Claim boundaries

- Contract-tested is not live-host runtime support.
- Local or fixture evidence is not external CI, cloud, scanner, or deployment
  evidence.
- Estimated tokens are not actual usage.
- Candidate learning is not curated learning.
- Protocol-ready evaluation/adoption is not measured efficacy or adoption.
- A passing test is not automatically architecture, behavior, migration,
  rollout, release, or root-cause proof.
